# EpochParticleEngine v0.2.0

Particle Studio turns the Vulkan lab from a fixed demo carousel into an interactive particle playground and scene builder, while the reference catalog grows from 9 to 15 deterministic scenes.

## Particle Studio

The first scene is now a live editor. Use the toolbar to place:

- Emitters
- Attractors
- Repulsors
- Vortices
- Circular collision obstacles
- Erase tool

Additional actions include burst, randomized scene generation, and clear. Hold **Shift** while placing for larger/stronger objects. Use **middle mouse** for an instant particle burst and **right mouse** for quick erase.

`Ctrl+S` saves the editable scene to `EpochParticleStudio.epscene`; `Ctrl+L` loads it again. Scene documents are validated before application and malformed documents do not mutate the current scene.

## New examples

- **Flow Field** — 5,200 particles advected through a changing vector field with live attract/repel steering.
- **Reaction Diffusion** — Gray-Scott chemistry with deterministic coral/maze patterns and paint/erase interaction.
- **Spring Cloth** — Verlet cloth with structural and shear constraints, mouse pulling, collision, and tearing.
- **Physarum Trails** — agent/grid slime-mold transport networks with chemotaxis and trail diffusion.
- **Weather Lab** — rain, snow, and hail sharing one particle pool with gusts, bounce, drag, and pointer-driven wind.

The existing fountain, cellular automata, Particle Life, Hybrid Sand, fire/smoke, fireworks, galaxy, boids, and SPH fluid scenes remain and now use a clearer showcase presentation.

## Navigation

- `1`–`9`: first nine scene shortcuts
- `Tab` / `Shift+Tab`: next / previous scene across all 15
- Click any scene in the side panel, including scenes 10–15
- `Space`: pause/resume
- `.`: single fixed step
- `R`: deterministic reset
- `-` / `+`: simulation speed
- `F1`: show/hide the side panel
- `Ctrl+S` / `Ctrl+L`: save/load the active editable scene

## Validation

v0.2.0 is required to pass the full catalog replay sweep and the repository's strict GCC, Clang, Apple Clang, Visual Studio 2026, ASan/UBSan, shared-package consumer, and Vulkan/vcpkg builds before publication.
